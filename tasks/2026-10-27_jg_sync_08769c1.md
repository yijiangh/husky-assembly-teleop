### 3.2 movement_exec and his latch

- `world_problem` (`sequences.py:149`): a soft-stopped robot refuses every part before its prompt and after Confirm:
  "<robot> is soft stopped; press Resume first".
- A command the robot refuses by raising (`MotionRefused`: the latch, or no lease) is REFUSED, nothing sent:
  `send_motion` (`send.py:231`, real and dry run), `robotiq_move` (`tools.py:100`), `scaffolding_grasp`'s close
  (`tools.py:175`, every tool still stopped on the way out). Before, it would have ended the part as FAILED
  ("the executor raised") with every arm held.
- movement_exec's task is spawned without `survives_soft_stop`: Stop all cancels it as before, recorded as Stop all
  (`cancelled_by`).

### 3.3 Guard 5: Stop all and a Confirm queued in the same tick

Before: the soft stop dropped queued intents, and the test asserted the Confirm was dropped. Now the monitor's
step 2 latches every robot and cancels the step's task; step 5 then runs the queued Confirm; step 6 runs the task,
which raises `CancelledError` at its prompt wait before the answer is read.
- `test_a_confirm_clicked_in_the_tick_of_stop_all_runs_after_it_and_moves_nothing`: the real `HuskyMonitor._soft_stop`
  and a real `PluginContext` queue. The Confirm runs (the queue is empty after), the step is CANCELLED by Stop all
  with a `soft_stop` event and no `confirm` event, no trajectory goes out (only holds would), both leases are free.
- `test_a_confirm_reaching_a_step_the_soft_stop_did_not_cancel_sends_nothing`: the latch set while the prompt waits,
  the task not cancelled (a clearance trip, or a task that survived): Confirm is refused by the check after it, with
  the Resume line; nothing sent, both leases free. If that check were missing, the send would still raise
  `RobotStopped` and be REFUSED (`test_a_soft_stopped_or_unleased_robot_refuses_and_nothing_goes_out`).
